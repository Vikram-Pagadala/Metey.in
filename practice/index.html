Sheet PR

Peer Review

An independent check of another consultant's structural design, offered at three levels. Each level includes everything in the one before.

To begin, we need the design basis report prepared by the main consultant and signed by the client.

I

Independent check

  • We build our own analysis model, in our own software.
  • We check the member sizes on the main consultant's drawings against gravity, wind and earthquake loads.
  • Where a section falls short, we say so and recommend a size.
Ends with
Certification of the design.
Terms
One-time. No part in later revisions or in construction.
II

Check and optimise

  • Everything in level I.
  • Member sizes optimised, in discussion with the main consultant and the client.
  • Drawings revised where needed, with the main consultant.
Ends with
Certification and a written report.
Terms
Two months at the least, depending on when drawings are issued.
III

Through construction

  • Everything in level II.
  • Consultancy continued through the construction period, where the client asks for it.
Ends with
The close of construction support.
Terms
Charged in addition to the review.
