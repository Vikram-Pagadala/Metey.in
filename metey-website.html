The Firm

Architects and structural engineers, founded in Hyderabad in 2013.

Committed to people. Committed to the future.

METEY Engineering and Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is an architectural, engineering, planning and management consultancy. It was set up in Hyderabad in 2013 and has a branch office in Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh.

We design multi-storey buildings for offices, commerce, education, healthcare, retail, housing and industry. We have prepared layouts for group housing across Andhra Pradesh, and provided architectural services for commercial, residential, office, educational, retail and industrial buildings.

Most buildings are now commissioned against a deadline, so we design around construction methods that shorten the programme: precast, monolithic, insulated wall, light-gauge steel and composite construction.
