Excellence in Structural Engineering, Integrity in Architectural Design.

METEY Engineering and Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a premier architectural, engineering, planning and project management consultancy. Established in Hyderabad in 2013, the practice maintains regional operations in Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, delivering projects nationwide.

We specialize in large-scale multi-storey developments across corporate offices, commercial centers, healthcare facilities, university campuses, mass housing, and specialized industrial plants.

Addressing demanding project timelines and economic targets, our practice leads the industry in implementing advanced precast, monolithic concrete, insulated structural panels, light-gauge steel, and composite construction technologies.
