Founder & Director

Er. C.A. Prasad

Director & Chief Structural Engineer

  • M.Tech (Engineering Structures), NIT Warangal, plus 3 years of research experience.
  • 48 years of experience in construction, design, quantity surveying and project management.
  • Worked in the Middle East for ten years with firms such as Balfour Beatty, WS Atkins and Engineers Office.
  • Involved in the design of the Burj Al Arab (56 storeys), Jumeirah Beach works, Millennium Grandstand, warehouses and 40–60 storey towers in Dubai, and the Ramada Tower in Doha.
  • Project management for the Passenger Terminal Building of Hyderabad International Airport.
  • Design of multi-storey towers in India, precast designs for villas and multi-storey structures, and design of industrial structures.
  • Promotes the adoption of modern construction technologies for the benefit of the construction industry, and advises the Government.
  • Fellow of the Institution of Engineers (India) (FIE); Fellow of ACCE(I); Chartered Engineer.
  • Member, American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE), USA; Chartered Member, Structural Engineering Institute (SEI), USA.
  • Founder, Secretary and Past President, Pre-Engineered Structures Society of India (PSI).
