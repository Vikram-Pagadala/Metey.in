About

Architecture & Structural Engineering Since 2013

Our company

Durable, Sustainable Structures, Built Sooner

Metey Engineering & Consultancy Pvt Ltd was established and registered in the combined Andhra Pradesh state in 2013, and has since been delivering projects across India.

  • Its core strengths are Architecture and Structural Engineering.
  • It focuses on using modern construction technologies — precast, monolithic (Mivan / aluminium formwork), composite, insulated wall (EPS / shotcrete), light gauge steel, Perma form and timber.
  • These technologies help build durable and sustainable structures in the shortest time, so buildings can be occupied earlier.
  • Metey follows Green Building principles in its designs.

Our history

From PS Engineering Consultant to Metey

  1. Before 2013

    The practice worked as PS Engineering Consultant.

  2. 2013

    Established and registered as Metey Engineering & Consultancy Pvt Ltd in the combined Andhra Pradesh state.

  3. Today

Areas of expertise

Founded by Er. C.A. Prasad

The firm was founded by Er. C.A. Prasad, who has 48 years of experience in:

  • Architecture — design of multi-storey towers (low-rise and high-rise), green buildings, earthquake-resistant design
  • Structures (Steel / RCC) for hotels, office buildings, shopping malls, godowns, workshops, warehouses, function halls and restaurants
  • MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing)
  • Infrastructure
  • FRP, Precast and Prestressed structures
