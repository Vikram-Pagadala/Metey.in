Technology 01

Precast construction

Factory-made concrete elements assembled on site — faster, economical, consistent quality. Used for housing, hostels, pharma and industrial buildings.

How it is built

Walls, slabs and frames cast off site and erected by crane.

Technology 02

Monolithic (Mivan / aluminium formwork)

Walls and slabs cast together in one pour — ideal for repetitive mass housing and high-rise towers.

How it is built

Walls and slabs cast together in aluminium formwork, floor after floor.

Technology 03

Composite construction

Structural steel frame with concrete deck / panels — light and quick to erect.

How it is built

Steel frames carrying hollow-core or deck slabs.

Projects by Metey · 2

  • Nursing School cum Hostel Block, Numaligarh, AssamNumaligarh, Golaghat, Assam
  • JSPL Buildings at Angul, Odisha — EPS Panel & CompositeAngul, Odisha

Technology 04

Insulated wall (EPS panel / shotcrete)

EPS core panels sprayed with concrete — thermal insulation and fast construction.

How it is built

EPS panels finished with sprayed concrete on both faces.

Technology 05

Light Gauge Steel (LGS)

Cold-formed steel framing for low-rise buildings.

How it is built

Cold-formed steel framing for light, quick structures.

Technology 06

Perma form

Permanent formwork panels that stay as part of the wall.

How it is built

Permanent formwork panels are set up and tied, then filled with concrete; the panels stay as the faces of the wall.

Technology 07

Polymer wall construction

Used for projects in Andhra Pradesh.

How it is built

Interlocking polymer wall panels are set up side by side and joined on site, then topped with the slab.

Technology 08

Timber / wooden structures

Glulam and timber structures (designed in Dubai, UAE).

How it is built

Glulam columns and rafters are raised as portal frames, joined with steel connectors and tied with timber purlins.
