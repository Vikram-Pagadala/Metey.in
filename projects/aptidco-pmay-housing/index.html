Government Housing

APTIDCO – PMAY Housing, Visakhapatnam & Vizianagaram

Architectural, structural and MEP services and preparation of township layouts for EWS housing under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY). Built using the Mivan (monolithic, aluminium formwork) construction method. Includes individual type designs such as the 365 sft unit (east- and west-facing).

Key facts

  • Flats of 300, 365 and 430 sft for low-income groups
  • Full township layouts with amenities
Client
APTIDCO (Andhra Pradesh Township and Infrastructure Development Corporation)
Location
