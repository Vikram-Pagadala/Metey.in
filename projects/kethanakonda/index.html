Affordable housing

Technology Demonstration Housing

A demonstration site for affordable (EWS) housing, where seven building systems were constructed at one location.

  1. Monolithic construction in aluminium formwork
  2. Insulated walls: EPS with sprayed concrete on both faces
  3. Precast walls with precast hollow-core slabs
  4. Composite construction: a structural steel frame with hollow-core slabs, and steel framing with deck-slab floors
  5. An EWS housing system from Malaysia
  6. An EWS housing system from Turkey
  7. An EWS housing system from China
